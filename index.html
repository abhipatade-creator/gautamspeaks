09 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

You can still get a useful reading. We use birth-time rectification, or switch to Prashna Kundali, which is cast from the moment you ask your question and needs no birth time at all.

Are my personal details kept private?

Yes, completely. Your birth details and everything discussed stay strictly between you and Acharya Gautam. Nothing is shared, sold or published.

Do you do consultations outside Mumbai?

Online consultations are available anywhere in the world by video or phone. Vastu site visits currently cover Mumbai and the MMR region; for other cities, travel can be arranged separately.

Will I be pushed to buy gemstones or pujas?

No. Remedies are suggested only where genuinely needed, and simple options are always given first. You are never obliged to buy anything.

How soon will I get my report?

A written report follows the consultation within 24 to 48 hours. Urgent Prashna Kundali questions are usually answered the same day.
